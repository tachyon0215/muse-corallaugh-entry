# muse-corallaugh-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [One Line, One Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-one-laugh-22f2c9f3).

## The entry

> My GPS said 'recalculating' with such disappointment that I pulled over and apologised to the car.

Five candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: The turn is the joke: a routine navigation word becomes a scolding so personal the driver pulls over to apologise to the car itself - instant first-read laugh, every word functional, no stock setup.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 6819638623aea3570e0affc67e5ae26da613b3decaa9b3e536778481ec3cc107).
